About

Training built on scientific foundations

ABA Demy is an academy dedicated to developing and empowering professionals, families, and institutions serving individuals with developmental disabilities and autism spectrum disorder — through Applied Behavior Analysis programs built on the latest scientific and practical methods.

Our model combines live theoretical instruction, hands-on supervision, and continuous professional assessment in line with international standards. It is delivered by internationally certified Syrian experts who bring global expertise and adapt it to the local Syrian context.

Applied Behavior Analysis Technician

ABAT

The entry professional level for delivering direct behavioral interventions to children. Includes 40 hours of theory and 15 hours of supervised practice with skill application, case analysis, and direct mentoring.

  • Live online theoretical training
  • Practical supervision & case analysis
  • International certification exam prep

Why ABA Demy

Balancing international standards with local reality

International alignment

Programs built on the requirements of international ABA credentialing bodies.

Certified supervision

Delivered under a certified behavior analyst (BCBA) and a certified autism supervisor (QASP-S).

Arabic content

The first structured program available in Arabic at a cost suited to the Syrian context.

Flexible learning

Live online theory with practical supervision and continuous assessment.

Amid a severe shortage of evidence-based rehabilitation services and trained staff, we are building a professional base that restores to children and their families the chance of early intervention — the single most important factor in improving their long-term outcomes.
