About

Training built on scientific foundations

ABA Demy is an Arabic-language platform specialized in Applied Behavior Analysis, combining specialized training, scientific resources, and a professional community — to meet practitioners' needs and support families with services and consultations grounded in best practice.

Our model combines live theoretical instruction, hands-on supervision, and continuous professional assessment in line with international standards. It is delivered by internationally certified Syrian experts who bring global expertise and adapt it to the local Syrian context.

Level One · Available now

Applied Behavior Analysis Technician

ABAT

The entry professional level for delivering direct behavioral interventions to children. Includes 40 hours of theory and 15 hours of supervised practice with skill application, case analysis, and direct mentoring.

  • Live online theoretical training
  • Practical supervision & case analysis
  • International certification exam prep

Why ABA Demy Was Founded

A story that began from a real need

01

The Reality

Amid rising demand for evidence-based interventions in Syria, there is a clear shortage of specialized training, Arabic-language resources, and professional services that practitioners and families need.

02

The Challenge

Practitioners faced limited opportunities for professional development and specialized qualification, while families struggled to access professional services to support their children with developmental disabilities early on.

03

This Is Where ABA Demy Began

To become one of the first specialized platforms in Syria, bringing together specialized training, educational resources, professional supervision, and family support services.
